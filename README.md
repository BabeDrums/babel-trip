## Current design and interaction (v1.7.0)

- **One day, one panel.** Seven continuously styled rounded panels: Tue Nov 17 preparation, Wed Nov 18 Arizona-to-California, Thu Nov 19 Covina family/evening, Fri Nov 20 Anaheim DJ night, Sat Nov 21 Grandpa/Huntington Beach, Sun Nov 22 Arizona return, plus **Trip total**. The top line of each panel shows day/date, a succinct location/context subtitle, and a **live day subtotal** (the last panel shows the grand total). One day panel is expanded at a time. On Nov 18–22 the matching calendar day opens initially; otherwise Wednesday opens as the next travel day.
- **Stops are concise by default, editable on demand.** Within the expanded day, successive stops are grouped in **one continuous white surface**, not multiple floating cards. A place's primary arrival/departure remains directly editable (native iPhone time input). Each multirow stop has a quiet one-tap detail disclosure giving the other original arrival/departure, duration, meal, parking, payment and expense controls; its short reactive summary remains visible when closed. Travel legs are only small, muted "10 min drive" connectors, not additional cards. Hide unverified "mi TBD" metadata from the default view; preserve essential known fuel details in their original content. No added aesthetic, badges, shadows, or unrelated shell.
- **Decisions stay functional.** Dizzy's Weekend/Friday/None changes tickets, fuel, routes and overnights. Mom-pays Rosalie, Saturday dinner/Grandpa, and optional Saturday karaoke remain functional inside the appropriate day. No user selection is forced by the new day panel. Any decision or editable cost must change both its detail and the day/grand totals correctly.
- **Clocks vs targets.** Arrival/departure values are estimates until manually corrected. An actual-time override holds its own checkpoint, and subsequent *unoverridden* estimates advance from it; old recorded times don't change on upstream edits. Fixed restaurant/event clock targets remain targets. The single quiet **Reset clocks** action appears after a manual clock correction and restores estimates, but not manually chosen stop durations, costs or attendance decisions. Edits **do not persist on page reload** yet; don't claim otherwise.
- **Status words are meaningful.** Wednesday's "Closes" shows time before/after the modeled Tacos Nogales midnight deadline; Friday "Check-in" shows minutes early/late to the preferred 7:30 PM arrival, while the 8 PM House of Blues DJ event stays fixed. All times, drive durations and event lengths remain planning estimates, not GPS/traffic guarantees.

## Runtime and maintenance

- `index.html` remains a self-contained HTML/CSS/JavaScript page. Existing system typography, palette variables, native editable clocks, discrete decisions, 56px actionable row rhythm, accessible 44px tap targets and light/dark responsiveness are retained. Each day-panel button exposes `aria-expanded`, each stop disclosure exposes `aria-expanded`, and choices use their existing accessible menu interactions.
- No automatic online ordering, restaurant reservations, real-time routing, payment authorizations, background tasks, cross-device sync, or claims of confirmed events. Any pre-order should be verified by the user with the restaurant; a published generic ordering link is not proof of a supported pickup.
- Before changing logic: inspect latest `main`, open PRs and docs, work on a separate branch, test **all three Dizzy scenarios**, dinner payer, karaoke, arrival and departure overrides, Cousin duration, taco pickup duration, AZ/PT timezone crossing, daily totals and grand total, and all accordion/disclosure states. Verify the exact successful GitHub Pages deployment after merging.
- **Next physical-device gate:** user reviews the single production URL on iPhone for panel scrolling, density, disclosure tap targets, legibility, light/dark and open menus. Keep specific fixes narrow. Revisit optional persistence only as a separate decision after observing actual travel usage.
